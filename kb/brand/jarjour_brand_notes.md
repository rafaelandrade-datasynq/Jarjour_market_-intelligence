# Jarjour Brand Notes

Use a sober, institutional and executive tone. The interface should look like a professional market intelligence tool, not a technical scraper console.

Prefer restrained contrast, clear hierarchy, compact tables and direct labels. Avoid playful copy, loud gradients and marketing-heavy composition.

The dashboard should communicate high standard, tradition, Brasilia market knowledge, sober sophistication, objectivity and trust. It should feel like an executive market intelligence product for corporate leasing and real estate opportunities.

Do not invent official logos or brand assets. Until official identity files exist, use neutral surfaces, near-black text and restrained amber or gold accents.

Visual polish must never hide data visibility. The dashboard must continue showing collected, normalized, raw-without-normalization, confidence and opportunity counts even when the layout is refined.

Avoid unnecessary charts. If a chart does not clarify Carol's decision workflow, leave it out.
