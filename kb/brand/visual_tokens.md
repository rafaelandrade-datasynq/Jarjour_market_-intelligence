# Visual Tokens

Initial visual direction:

- Background: warm off-white or neutral stone.
- Primary text: near black zinc.
- Accent: restrained gold or amber.
- Borders: soft neutral gray.
- Radius: 6px to 8px.
- Tables: dense, readable, with clear status chips.
- Layout: generous whitespace, executive hierarchy and no decorative clutter.
- Cards: soft borders, white or warm-white surfaces, restrained shadows.
- Actions: near-black primary buttons with clear labels.
- Dashboard density: enough information for repeated work, without spreadsheet clutter.

These are provisional tokens until the Jarjour identity is formalized.

The interface must not look like a technical scraping console. Avoid loud colors, heavy gradients, excessive charts and purely decorative elements.
