# Carol Problem

Carol researches real estate market opportunities manually every month. She searches portals, applies filters such as neighborhood, property type, sale or rent purpose, area and price, then moves information into Excel.

Her spreadsheet tracks address, type, area, rent, condominium fee, contact, notes and links. The manual process is repetitive, hard to audit and loses historical intelligence over time.

The product must preserve Carol's practical workflow while improving it with history, confidence classification, opportunity ranking and an executive dashboard.
