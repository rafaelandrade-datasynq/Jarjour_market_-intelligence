# ADR-002: Django Templates, HTMX And Tailwind

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

The MVP uses Django Templates, HTMX and Tailwind CSS for the frontend.

## Rationale

The product needs a focused internal interface with low operational complexity. Django Templates and HTMX keep the stack simple while supporting rich enough interactions.

## Consequences

Do not introduce React or Streamlit in this project without a new decision.
